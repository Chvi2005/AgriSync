# 🚀 AgriSync — Future Roadmaps & Production Scaling Plans

## 1. Short-Term Enhancements (Q3–Q4 2026)
- **Offline SMS / USSD Gateway (Dialog / Mobitel Agri-Services):** Enable rural smallholders without smartphones or 4G data to list harvest yields, receive match SMS alerts, and accept purchase orders via simple USSD codes (e.g., `*880#`).
- **Sinhala & Tamil Multilingual Localization:** Full localized UI with voice-assisted input for farmers in local languages.
- **Automated Weather & Pest Warning Webhooks:** Direct integration with Department of Meteorology Sri Lanka to issue proactive crop harvest advisories.

## 2. Mid-Term Commercial Scale (2027)
- **Escrow-Backed Digital Settlements:** Instant UPI / LankaQR direct-to-bank settlement escrow accounts to prevent non-payment risks.
- **Shared Cold-Chain Fleet Telematics:** IoT GPS and temperature sensors installed in refrigerated logistics trucks with real-time temperature tracking from farm gate to supermarket shelf.
- **Carbon Credit Tokenization:** Verifiable carbon reduction calculations based on localized transport routing and reduced crop decomposition emissions.

## 3. Long-Term Vision
- Expanding the AgriSync autonomous multi-agent brokering model across South Asia to support cross-border agricultural commodity trade.
