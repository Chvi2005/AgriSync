OPEN CATEGORY

PROTOTYPE GUIDELINES

Each team must submit a Video Demonstration and the GitHub Repo of an almost completed early
version of their application based on the original proposal

Your application must include a functioning AI Agent , not just a chatbot or single-prompt wrapper.
It must demonstrate real reasoning, decision-making, or action-taking (e.g., tool use, memory,
multi-step workflows, planning) that meaningfully contributes to solving the core problem.

You may use any existing AI model or framework; it doesn't need to be built from scratch.

Ensure proper licensing for any third-party tools, models, or APIs used. We recommend open-
source or well-documented options such as OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini APIs, LangChain,

MediaPipe, or Hugging Face

Evaluation will consider how closely the execution aligns with your initial idea.
A panel of judges will shortlist teams based on the video and GitHub Repo submitted

OPEN CATEGORY

PROTOTYPE GUIDELINES

Live data is not required at this stage. You may use hard-coded values or dummy data to
showcase your design and approach but the agent logic itself (Open Category) must be
real and running, not simulated to look like an agent.
As there will be limited time after the prototype results, we highly encourage you to
complete most of the application by this stage.
After submitting the prototype, teams can continue refining and improving the same
application for later stages.
Include a brief explanation of your future plans for the application and how you intend to
enhance it in the next phase.
Any pivots from the original proposal (tech stack, features, or agent behavior) must be clearly
explained and justified.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Upload a video (screen recording) demonstrating and explaining how the application works. (Cannot
exceed 5 minutes)

You must navigate through your application, display each page/feature, and use voiceover to
explain the purpose and functionalities included. Open Category teams must clearly show the AI
Agent in action (its inputs, steps, and outputs).

You may record your video using the Zoom platform. The presenter should be clearly audible and
visible on screen. Casual attire is allowed.

Language of use –> English.

The video file must be named "[Team Name]_Video_Pitch".

Please ensure your submitted GitHub repository includes a detailed README.md file that clearly
outlines your project's purpose, the tech stack used (which must match your proposal), setup
instructions, and your core features, including the AI agent workflow for Open Category teams.
